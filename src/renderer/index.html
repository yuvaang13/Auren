welcome to auren

A private guardrail for your AI agents.

Auren blocks specific websites from being reached by anything on this computer — including AI agents — until you turn it off with your fingerprint or passcode.

It works below every browser and app, at the network level, so it can’t be skipped by opening a different browser. While protection is on, it also blocks terminal and OS-level network access (ping, ssh, FTP, cloud metadata).

your block list

Add a site below and Auren makes it unreachable everywhere on this computer. Changes apply instantly — no save button.

Turning protection off requires your fingerprint or device passcode, and then keeps sites blocked for 10 seconds while you confirm — so an AI agent can’t turn it off on its own.

While protection is on, auren also hides your keystrokes: this window can’t be captured on screen, and keystroke-reading agents can’t intercept what you type here.
